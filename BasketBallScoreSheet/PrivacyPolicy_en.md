This Privacy Policy describes how yukitaka13 ("we", "our", or "us"), the developer of the mobile application "Basketball Scoresheet" (the "App"), collects, uses, and shares information about users ("you" or "User") of the App.

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or California (USA), please also refer to the region-specific sections below.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

### a. Information You Provide
- Scores, member rosters, team information, and other data you enter in the App (stored locally on your device; not transmitted to our servers).
- Information you voluntarily provide when contacting us (name, email address, content of inquiry).
- Photos of member lists that you take or select when you use the Member List Reading feature (sent to Google; see Section 4).

### b. Information Collected Automatically
- Advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS, AAID on Android) and aggregated usage data, collected by third-party advertising networks (free version only).
- Device type, operating system version, and crash logs, for diagnostic and quality improvement purposes.
- App and device integrity information used to prevent abuse of the Member List Reading feature (Firebase App Check; see Section 4).

### c. Payment-Related Information
- Subscription status, receipt data, and transaction identifiers issued by Apple Inc. (App Store) or Google LLC (Google Play).
- **We do not collect, process, or store your credit card number, billing address, or any other payment information.** All payment processing is handled directly by Apple or Google in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

## 4. Member List Reading

When you use the "Read member list" feature, the photo of a member list that you take or select is sent to a generative AI service provided by Google LLC (the Gemini API, accessed through Firebase AI Logic) to read the jersey numbers and player names written on it.

- Before your first use, the App asks you to confirm that the photo will be sent to Google's service.
- We do not receive or store the photo or the recognition results on our servers. Only the entries that you review and select are saved to the member list on your device.
- Photos are processed under the terms for Google's paid services and are not used to improve Google's products, including training AI models. Google may retain submitted content for a limited period to detect abuse. For details, see the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.
- Photos may contain the names of players, including minors and players of other teams. Please obtain consent from the people concerned where appropriate.
- Photos may be processed in Google's facilities in the United States and other countries.
- The number of times you use the feature is recorded on your device and is not sent to our servers.
- To prevent abuse of the feature, the App uses Google's Firebase App Check, which sends information used to verify that the app and device are genuine (Apple App Attest on iOS, Google Play Integrity on Android) to Google.

## 5. Advertising

The free version of the App displays advertisements served by third-party advertising networks. These networks may use cookies and advertising identifiers (IDFA/AAID) to deliver interest-based ads. Such information is collected anonymously and is not used to personally identify you.

Users who purchase the Ad Removal Plan will not see advertisements within the App.

You may limit the use of advertising identifiers via your device settings:
- iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking
- Android: Settings > Google > Ads
