Penny Pup Privacy Policy

Effective: November 12, 2026

This policy explains how yukitaka13 ("the developer", "we") handles information in the app Penny Pup (called まねきち in Japanese; "the app").

1. About the app

Penny Pup is a pretend wallet that lets parents and kids keep track of chores and allowance together. It does not handle real money, bank accounts or cards. A parent creates the family, and a child's phone joins it by scanning a QR code shown on the parent's phone.

2. Information we collect and why

InformationWhy
The parent's email address, password and app languageTo sign in, send verification codes by email and confirm it's you. Passwords are protected by the sign-in service and we can't see them.
Family records: family name, the names the family uses for each parent, each child's nickname, color and display style, chore names, amounts and schedules, allowance settings, the amounts and times of deposits and payments, balances, currency and time zoneTo run the app and show the same information on every phone in the family.
Device information: a device ID created by the app, a push notification token (issued by Apple or Google) and the operating systemTo link phones to the family and to send notifications, such as when a chore is approved or money arrives.
Purchase information: the App Store or Google Play transaction ID, subscription status and expiry dateTo check whether the family's subscription is active. We never receive card numbers or other payment details.
A value derived from your IP address that can't be turned back into the addressTo stop people from guessing family join codes. It is deleted automatically after about 10 minutes.
Logs, such as error details and timestampsTo investigate problems. They are deleted automatically after 14 days.

3. What we don't collect or do

  • We don't collect location, contacts or photos.
  • The camera is used only to scan QR codes. Nothing it sees is saved or sent.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • We don't sell your information or use it for advertising.

4. Children's information

  • Children never enter an email address or password. A parent enters each child's nickname and settings. A nickname is fine; you don't need to use a real name.
  • A child's phone joins the family only when a parent shows a QR code from their own phone. Parents can unlink a child's phone at any time.
  • Parents can review, change or delete their children's information at any time. Removing a child from the family deletes that child's records.
  • If you believe a child's information was collected without a parent's involvement, contact us and we will delete it.

5. Service providers

We don't share your information with anyone except where required by law. To run the app, we use these providers:

  • Amazon Web Services: data storage and servers (Tokyo region), and verification emails
  • Apple: notifications to iPhone (Apple Push Notification service) and App Store subscriptions
  • Google: notifications to Android (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and Google Play subscriptions

6. Where and how long we keep information

  • Information is stored in Japan (the Amazon Web Services Tokyo region). If you use the app outside Japan, your information is transferred to and stored in Japan.
  • We keep it while your account exists. Deleted information may remain in disaster-recovery backups for up to 35 days before it is removed.

7. Deleting your account and information

In the app, go to Settings and tap “Delete account”.

  • If another parent is in the family: your account and your phone's information are deleted. The family's and children's records stay with the remaining parent.
  • If you are the last parent: all family and children's records, and the children's phone information, are deleted.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel your subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Each family's data is kept separate so no family can see another family's information.

9. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page.
