Penny Pup: delete your account and data

How to delete your account and its data in Penny Pup (called まねきち in Japanese; developer: yukitaka13).

Delete in the app

  1. Open Penny Pup on the parent's phone.
  2. Go to Settings, tap “Delete account” and confirm.

Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app, email yukitaka13.1110@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with and write “Delete my account”. We will confirm it's you and delete your data within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • If another parent is in the family: your account (such as your email address) and your phone's information (device ID and notification token). The family's and children's records stay with the remaining parent.
  • If you are the last parent: all of the above, plus all family records (family and children's names, chores, allowance settings, deposits and payments, balances) and the children's phone information.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Disaster-recovery backups: removed within 35 days.
  • Logs: deleted after 14 days.
  • Subscription records stay with the App Store or Google Play. Deleting your account doesn't cancel your subscription, so cancel it in your store's subscription settings.
