Thanks for using Penny Pup. Kids earn by doing chores and pay at your pretend checkout with a QR code. It's a family allowance app that never uses real money.

FAQ

Does it use real money?
No. Money in Penny Pup is practice money for your family only. It never connects to a bank account or card.
How do I link my child's phone?
On your phone, tap “Add a child” to show a QR code. On your child's phone, open Penny Pup, choose the kid option and scan that code. Kids don't need an email address or password.
Can another parent use it too?
On your phone, go to Settings and tap “Invite a grown-up” to show a QR code. The other parent signs in to Penny Pup on their phone, taps “Join a family” and scans the code. They can enter the 6-digit code instead if scanning doesn't work.
My child got a new phone or lost theirs
On your phone, open your child's page and tap “Unlink” next to the old device. Their balance and history stay. Then scan the QR code again on the new phone.
Does allowance arrive if nobody opens the app?
Yes. Our server pays it in automatically on the day you set.
Notifications aren't arriving
Check that notifications are allowed for Penny Pup. On iPhone, go to Settings › Notifications › Penny Pup. On Android, go to Settings › Apps › Penny Pup › Notifications.
How much does it cost?
Your first month is free. After that, it's a monthly auto-renewing subscription; the price for your country is shown in the store before you subscribe. One subscription covers the whole family, however many kids you have. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or current period ends and you won't be charged.
How do I cancel?
On iPhone, go to Settings › your name › Subscriptions. On Android, open Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions. Deleting your account in the app doesn't cancel the subscription.
How do I delete my account and data?
On your phone, go to Settings and tap “Delete account”. If you're the last parent in the family, all family and children's records are deleted too. See section 7 of the Privacy Policy for details.
